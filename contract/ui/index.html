FAQ

Questions people ask

ZEEP maps a handle to a tNIGHT receiving address on-chain. Enter a handle, review its address, then approve the wallet transfer.

No password or email. Connect a Midnight wallet, then register a handle. Keep access to that wallet to receive payments.

The directory publishes handle hashes and receiving addresses. Native tNIGHT is unshielded, so payment amounts and destinations are visible on-chain.

Yes. Enter their handle in Send or open their payment link. ZEEP resolves the address on-chain. Confirm that address with the recipient before approving.

The directory allows one handle per supplied address. Its demo contract cannot prove the registrant owns that address, so a direct caller could claim someone else's address.

ZEEP reads your unshielded address from 1AM and uses it to find your registered handle. The app checks a wallet signature when you register; the contract itself cannot verify that signature yet.

A handle payment reaches the recipient's unshielded wallet after network confirmation. There is no ZEEP claim step. Check the confirmed transaction and recipient balance.

Your wallet handles the network fee when you register or send. The app shows an error if the wallet has no usable balance for the transfer.
