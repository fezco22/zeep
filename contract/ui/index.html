How it works

Three circuits, one private rail

Each step is a zero-knowledge circuit call on Midnight. Nothing here links a sender to a receiver to an amount.

register

Pay a handle

Amount and payer stay private witnesses. Only an opaque commitment lands on-chain.

claim

Claim with proof

Prove the note is yours and publish a nullifier. A second claim of the same note is rejected.

FAQ

          
What the chain can and cannot see

          

            What can an observer learn?
            
That a payment happened, the total note count, and the public username directory. Never who paid, how much, which receiver a note belongs to, or which note a claim spent.

          

          

            Why is the username directory public?
            
Discoverability is the product: you share a handle so people can pay it. Privacy lives in the note, commitment, and nullifier layer, not in the directory.

          

          

            How is a double claim blocked without a public balance table?
            
Claiming a note publishes a nullifier derived from your secret and the note. A second claim of the same note recomputes the same nullifier, which is already present, so the contract rejects it.

          

          

            How does the receiver learn a note is theirs?
            
In this MVP the payer hands over a claim code out of band (the commitment plus amount and salt). On-chain encrypted payloads are a planned upgrade.

          

          

            Is this audited?
            
No. ZEEP runs on Midnight Preprod (testnet) and is not audited. Do not use it with real funds.
