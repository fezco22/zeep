# Feedback process

ZEEP uses short, task-based sessions to find problems in handle registration, recipient lookup, sending, receiving, and wallet activity.

## Session

1. Tell the participant that the app is on Midnight Preprod and native tNIGHT transfers and wallet addresses are public.
2. Ask them to connect a test wallet and complete one task, such as registering a handle or sending a small amount to a recipient they can verify.
3. Ask the questions below. Never request a seed phrase, private key, or wallet password.
4. Record the date, task, outcome, issue, and proposed next step. Record a wallet address or explorer link only when needed to verify an interaction and the participant agrees to share it.
5. Review submissions regularly. Group issues by onboarding, registration, sending, receiving, and documentation. Prioritize by impact and recurrence.
6. Record the decision and link any resulting code or documentation change. Follow up with the participant when a fix changes their original task.

## Questions

- What were you trying to do?
- Which step was unclear or did not work?
- Did the handle resolve to the address you expected?
- Did the wallet show the recipient and amount you expected?
- Could you verify the transfer afterward?
- How confident are you that the payment went to the right person? (1–5)
- What one change would make the task easier?
- May we share your public wallet address and transaction link as evidence of this session?
